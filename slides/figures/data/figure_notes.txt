DISTRIBUTIONS AND JOINT TABLE
Same eligible article denominator across all measures. Zero includes neutral, mixed and insufficient directional evidence. The joint table conditions reputation on alignment; rows sum to 100%, and this is not a confusion matrix or an accuracy test. The correlation treats -1/0/+1 as equally spaced scores and is descriptive; article and source dependence remain. 20.1% of supporting actions are reputation-negative; these are coding implications, not measured changes in public reputation.
