DISTRIBUTIONS AND JOINT TABLE
Same eligible article denominator across all measures. Zero includes neutral, mixed and insufficient directional evidence. The correlation treats -1/0/+1 as equally spaced scores and is descriptive; article and source dependence remain. 20.8% of supporting actions are reputation-negative; these are coding implications, not measured changes in public reputation.
