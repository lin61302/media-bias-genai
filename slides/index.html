The political meaning of a negative report

A report can describe government abuse without adding hostile editorial language.

Coercion against peaceful opponents may strengthen an incumbent’s political position while damaging its legitimacy.

The same event can receive descriptive coverage, endorsement, or condemnation.

These distinctions matter for studying accountability, media control, and the information citizens receive.

Conceptual illustration. Definitions: complete prompt, October 20 release.2
Speaker notes
This is a conceptual motivation, not an empirical claim that any particular outlet or country behaves this way. A single positive/negative sentiment label can combine fundamentally different relationships. Our question is what is reported and how it is presented. Effects on audiences need additional evidence.

Two comparisons for studying media control

Which events receive coverage?

Compare the selection and prominence of government-relevant actions across outlets.

Needs a common event or exposure denominator.

How is the same event presented?

Compare narrator evaluation within matched events, government targets, and time periods.

Needs joint article-level measures and credible event matching.

Ownership and regulatory variation become explanatory variables after measurement and comparability checks.

Research-design proposal; literature in references/references.bib.11
Speaker notes
Frame does not itself recover gatekeeping or source-selection bias. Event matching separates variation in what happened from variation in presentation, but matching quality and article completeness still matter. Ownership or regulation associations remain observational without an identification strategy. An ownership change can be studied within outlets if the timing and counterfactual are defensible.

The smaller reviews were useful for discovering failure mechanisms, including scope, action selection, entity targets, and translation defects. A validation design for academic claims

  1. Freeze the taskPrompt, translation policy, incumbent scope, and evaluation rubric.
  2. Sample the difficult and common casesCountries, languages, source types, action categories, nulls and all label directions.
  3. Code independently before reconciliationBlinded human judgments, original-language checks, and recorded disagreements.
  4. Report field-specific performanceConfusion matrices, classwise recall/precision, uncertainty and subgroup failures.
Proposed protocol: docs/validation.md.14
Speaker notes
Separate a development set from held-out evaluation, and report original coder agreement before adjudication. Oversample rare labels and uncertain reference cases for diagnosis, but reweight or separately report prevalence-sensitive metrics. Evaluate gov identity and actor/target extraction in addition to label signs. Prompt revisions require new versioned evaluations. See the validation document for the complete proposed protocol.

Speaker notes
Invite criticism of the measurement construct as well as suggestions for design. In particular, the reasonable-observer criterion can conceal disagreement about rights, performance and legitimacy. Consider whether those disagreements should be coded separately, rather than forcing agreement.
